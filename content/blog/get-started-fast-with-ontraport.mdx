They say "the more you tell, the more you sell" — which is true, but only if people actually read what you write. People read text that is punchy, interesting, and helps them make a good buying decision.

The same idea applies to Ontraport: more campaigns do not equal better results if the foundations are messy.

## Start with foundations

Before you build a 20-step automation, get these right:

1. **Clean contact fields** — know what you are capturing and why.
2. **Simple tags** — mark source, interest, and lifecycle stage.
3. **One welcome journey** — a short sequence that teaches, earns trust, and points to a next step.

## Write emails people finish

Long emails are fine when every line earns its place. Prefer:

- One idea per email
- A clear reason to care in the first two lines
- One primary call to action

## Then automate with intention

Once your welcome path works, expand into:

- Abandoned cart or incomplete application follow-ups
- Re-engagement for cool leads
- Post-purchase onboarding

Speed comes from clarity, not from packing every feature into week one.
