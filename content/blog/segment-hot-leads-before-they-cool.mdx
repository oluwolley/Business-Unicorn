If every contact gets the same email, your hottest leads cool off while inactive subscribers drain deliverability.

## The problem with one-size-fits-all

Unsegmented lists create three expensive problems:

- Interested buyers get generic content
- Cold contacts ignore you (or mark you as spam)
- You cannot tell what is working

## A simple segmentation starter kit

You do not need a complex taxonomy on day one. Start with three tag types:

1. **Source** — webinar, opt-in, referral, checkout
2. **Interest** — product, service, or topic they chose
3. **Temperature** — new, engaged, quiet, customer

## Match the message to the moment

Once tags exist, your automations can respond with relevance:

- New + interested → education and proof
- Engaged + not booked → direct offer or call CTA
- Quiet for 60 days → re-engagement or sunset path

Segmentation is not about sending more email. It is about sending the right email while the lead is still warm.
